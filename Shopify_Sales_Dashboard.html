Key Insights
  • Revenue grew 30% across the week ($577K → $751K) but wasn't linear — Fri and Sun both dropped 15%+ before rebounding.
  • Running, Tennis, and Walking Shoes drive 71% of revenue, while seven of fourteen categories each contribute under 2%.
  • 46% of customers return within the same week, with a median gap of just 43.6 hours to a second order — a short, high-leverage retention window.
  • Revenue concentration is moderate — the top 20% of customers drive 38% of revenue, so growth is broad-based, not whale-dependent.
  • Order frequency drops sharply after order #2 (1,322 customers at 2 orders vs. only 36 at 5+) — converting first-timers to second-timers matters more than chasing loyalists further.
